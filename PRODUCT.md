# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Small businesses and entrepreneurs, particularly in the Montreal area, who need a professional web presence.

## Product Purpose
GDG Productions creates websites that help small businesses and entrepreneurs present their work and services online.

## Positioning
Custom-designed websites that make each client's business memorable. This is the differentiator the founder chose to emphasize.

## Operating Context
The public website is in French and invites prospective clients to explore services or request a project discussion. The business is based in Montreal, Canada.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Website showcases, portfolios, responsive web design, and custom interface design are advertised services.
- The site advertises SEO optimization and fast loading, but no measurable performance or search-ranking guarantee is established.
- The current site names Astro and React as implementation technologies.
- The contact form uses an optional `PUBLIC_CONTACT_API_URL` endpoint and includes consent disclosures for Quebec privacy requirements.
- Do not add services, timelines, prices, guarantees, or client outcomes that the founder has not confirmed.

## Brand Commitments
- Preserve the GDG Productions name and the existing diamond logo.
- The existing brand story connects charcoal, transformation, and a revealed diamond.
- The founder authorized a new visual direction and messaging emphasis, while asking that the business's actual domains and competencies remain accurate.

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing logo: `shaky-star/public/logogdgproductions.jpg`.
- The website and README describe the services and technologies listed above.
- The repository includes brand-story artwork, but no verified client case studies or testimonials. Existing portfolio tiles are illustrative placeholders and must not be presented as completed client work.

## Product Principles
- Make the service immediately relevant to small businesses and entrepreneurs.
- Show how custom web design can make a business easier to discover and remember, without promising unverified outcomes.
- Keep project inquiries and contact details easy to find.
- Prefer concrete demonstrations of web craft over generic agency claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Keep the French-language experience readable and usable with keyboard navigation, reduced-motion settings, and mobile layouts.
